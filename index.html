Meet the owner

Will Maginnis

Owner Will Maginnis is a graduate of Upper Cape Cod Technical High School's Horticulture and Landscaping shop. Here he learned landscape construction techniques and found a passion for creating enjoyable outdoor living areas.

Will was the winner of the state FFA hardscape competition freshman year and began building patios, walkways, and other hardscape features for his customers around Sagamore Beach MA. where he grew up.

Will now holds an associate degree in Landscape Contracting from UMass Amherst's Stockbridge School of Agriculture.
